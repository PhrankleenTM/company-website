Who We Are

A fully
integrated
extrusion plant.

Rotic Aluminium Extrusion is a fully integrated aluminium extrusion manufacturing company located in Awka, Anambra State. Established in 2023, our factory is purpose-built to redefine aluminium extrusion through precision, sustainability and craftsmanship.

At Rotic Aluminium, we transform locally sourced recycled aluminium scrap into high-performance profiles used in construction, architecture, interior design and industrial fabrication.

From melting and casting to extrusion, aging and powder coating, every stage of our production reflects quality, consistency and attention to detail.
