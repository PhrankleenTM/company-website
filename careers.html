Awka, Anambra State · Full-time, On-site
  • Shift Production Manager Entry · Mid Level

    We're hiring Shift Production Managers in Awka, Anambra State. This role involves overseeing production activities and ensuring operational efficiency.

    Key Responsibilities

    • Supervise production activities during assigned shifts.
    • Monitor and control production processes to maximize efficiency and meet production targets.
    • Share daily reports with the management team on production metrics, downtimes and performance during the shift.
    • Facilitate seamless transition between production operations during shift changes.
    • Ensure production output is consistent with quality standards.
    • Maintain production documentation and oversee equipment operation and maintenance.
    • Manage and coordinate the work of production workers.
    • Ensure compliance with company policies and regulatory requirements.
    • Ensure adequate staffing and training to support ongoing production.

    Skills and Qualifications

    • Minimum 2 years of experience as a shift production manager in a manufacturing industry or similar experience.
    • Attention to detail, critical thinking and computer literacy.
    • Proficiency in Excel for generating timely reports.
    • Effective communication skills and ability to work night shifts.
    • Excellent crisis management and adaptation skills.
    • Flexibility to work day or night shifts in a fast-paced environment.

    Additional Preference

    We strongly encourage candidates who are willing and able to live onsite in the factory accommodation in Awka.

    Benefits We Offer

    • Comfortable accommodation.
    • A free meal daily.
    • Solid bonus system.
    • Medical bills coverage.
